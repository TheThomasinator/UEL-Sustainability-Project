BUSINESSES Advancing knowledge, shaping the future...

Businesses play a vital role in driving sustainable growth and strengthening local communities, contributing expertise, resources, and innovative thinking across a wide range of sectors. Our business partners engage in meaningful collaboration, from participating in one‑to‑one advisory sessions to improve their environmental performance, to supporting pioneering sustainability initiatives that benefit the wider region. They also work alongside researchers, stakeholders, and community groups to develop impactful projects, share insights, and produce reports and publications that help inform and inspire the public .

This page enables administration staff to manage business profiles, ensuring that all company information remains accurate, relevant, and up‑to‑date. The details of current business partners can also be accessed and explored using the search tools below, allowing staff to quickly locate specific organisations, review their involvement, and maintain strong, ongoing relationships.
