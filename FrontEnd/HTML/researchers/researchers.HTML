RESEARCHERS
Advancing knowledge, shaping the future...

Researchers are the driving force behind innovation and sustainable solutions, fostering collaboration and knowledge sharing across disciplines and throughout the wider community. Our researchers carry out a range of work, such as holding one-to-one meetings with business representatives to advise them in making their business more sustainable, pioneering cutting edge sustainability projects, alongside various partners in aid of the wider community, and coming together to write papers and publications to share with the public.

This page allows administration staff to manage researcher profiles, ensuring that their information remains accurate and up-to-date. The details of current researchers can also be accessed and searched through using the search tools below.
