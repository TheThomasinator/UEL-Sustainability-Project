Driving research into a sustainable impact...

Projects are the work of researchers and partners, collaborating to develop sustainable solutions to real-world problems.
Each project represents the combined effort to approach and attempt resolving real-world challenges, by mixing researchers' academic input with the funding, resources, & support of partner organisations.
Through these projects, researchers & partners can work hand-in-hand, growing stronger connections across businesses, speading a positive impact to be shared amongst closely working organisations.
This page allows the managing of project records, to ensure details such as timelines, summaries, & associated partners, remain accurate & up to date.
