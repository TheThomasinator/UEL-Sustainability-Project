AREAS
Key details for every location, postcode, environment rating, and area name in one place.

The Areas page provides a structured overview of locations linked to businesses within the system. Each area displays key information such as postcode, environmental status, and area name, allowing users to compare locations and understand regional differences. This section supports analysis of how different areas align with sustainability goals and helps users identify patterns across locations in a clear and organised format.
